# Language book expansion: source inventory and editorial proposal

Date: 2026-10-25. Register: FOR DOMINIK. Status: proposal, no manuscript changes.

User requested additional Medium/Metaphor Hacker essays, a better title, and an Opus visual design consultation. Public author pages were searched and opened; this inventory identifies source candidates, not independently verified linguistic claims.

## Located sources

- 17 Things Everyone Should Know About Language But Doesn’t Even Know to Ask, Medium, 11 March 2018. Opening candidate: broad accessible introduction. Source retains a numbering irregularity (two items numbered 12); preserve original in archive and propose any correction through the editorial workflow.
- 5 things everybody should know about language: Outline of linguistics' contribution to the liberal arts curriculum, Metaphor Hacker, 1 January 2014. Longer companion on education. Opening explicitly calls it a draft. Compare overlap with 17 Things before inclusion; do not silently combine or present as revised author text.
- Why Chomsky doesn't count as a gifted linguist, Metaphor Hacker, 1 August 2010. Exact published title is narrower than the user's recalled wording. Essay includes discussion of Halliday, Jakobson, Fillmore, Labov, Hoey, Croft, Lakoff and Langacker; a separate important-linguists article has not yet been identified. Retain as an attributed author argument, not a neutral factual headline invented by the editor.
- Linguistics according to Fillmore, Metaphor Hacker, 15 February 2014. Candidate alongside the preceding essay, with publication date and personal-perspective postscript preserved. Do not add a new biography or silently modernise historical language.
- 10 ways in which music is like language and 8 (more important) ways in which it is not, Medium, 19 March 2018. Optional additional essay. Source explicitly says it began as a Stack Exchange answer, then Tumblr, and was expanded: compare against the 253 existing answers before duplicating it.

## Provisional book structure

1. What everyone should know about language: accessible opening essays, with duplication assessed.
2. Questions about language: retain all 253 existing answers, current nine topic groups and stable identifiers.
3. Ways of thinking about language: Chomsky critique and Fillmore essay; important linguists represented by actual source text, not invented new chapters.
4. Source/publication notes and references: preserve original provenance per piece and distinguish later accepted edits.

Order and inclusion remain proposals for author review. Do not import into WriteFlex or rewrite canonical manuscripts during source discovery. Later additions should enter the existing canonical Folio editorial process and export pipeline, with original dates, images/quote provenance and version/deduplication checks.

## Title candidates, not selected

Recommended working direction: Language Beyond Grammar — Essays and Answers About How Language Works.
Alternatives: What Everyone Should Know About Language — Essays, Questions and Answers; Making Sense of Language — Essays and Answers from a Linguist.

These are editorial suggestions based on the expanded scope, not author-approved wording or originality/market-clearance claims. Stack Exchange belongs in provenance/subtitle notes rather than dominating a title for a mixed-source book. Keep existing book identifiers and URLs stable if display title changes.
