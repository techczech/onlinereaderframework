W3C’s explanation of WCAG 2.2 criterion 1.4.13 requires authored hover content to remain visible when the pointer moves onto it. Dismissal without moving hover or focus is required when the content obscures or replaces meaningful content. Tooltips rendered by the browser itself have an exception; these CSS tooltips are authored. This review relies on the unconditional hoverability requirement and separately records the requested Escape behaviour. It does not presume that every tooltip overlaps meaningful content.
