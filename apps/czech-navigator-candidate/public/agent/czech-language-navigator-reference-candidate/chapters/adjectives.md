<h1 id="reader-content-adjectives">Adjectives</h1>
<p>There are two types of adjectives in Czech. One type ends in a <em>hard consonant</em> <strong>+ ý, á, é</strong>; and the other in a <em>soft consonant</em> <strong>+ í</strong> in the Kdo? Co? case.</p>
<h2 id="reader-content-hard-adjectives">Hard Adjectives</h2>
<p>Adjectives where the last consonant is: <strong><em>-h-, -ch-, -k-, -r-, -d-, -t-, -n-</em></strong> (see <em>Pronunciation</em> for more details).</p>

<h2 id="reader-content-how-to-form-adjectives">How to Form Adjectives</h2>
<ul>
<li><p>Adjectives are words that describe properties of other objects.</p></li>
<li><p>The question word for adjectives is <em>jaký?</em> vs. <em>co?</em> for nouns.</p></li>
<li><p>Adjectives can be either genuine (<em>nový dům</em> = new house) or derived (<em>zeleninová pizza</em> = vegetable pizza)</p></li>
</ul>
<h3 id="reader-content-adjectives-not-formed-from-other-parts-of-speech">Adjectives not formed from other parts of speech</h3>
<p>Most adjectives describe properties of other objects by themselves, and are not derived from other words:</p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>malý</strong> (small), <strong>velký</strong> (large), <strong>celý</strong> (whole), žlutý (yellow), <strong>bílý</strong> (white), <strong>nový</strong> (new), <strong>tvrdý</strong> (hard), <strong>veselý</strong> (cheerful), <strong>zdravý</strong> (healthy)…</p>
</blockquote>

<h2 id="reader-content-possessive-adjectives">Possessive adjectives</h2>
<p>In English, possession is expressed by –’<em>s</em>. For instance, <em>John’s father, Jana’s book</em>. In Czech, endings <strong>-ův</strong> or <strong>-in</strong> are used as in <em>Johnův otec</em> - <em>Janina kniha</em>. They take different forms in different cases. See below. The only reason they are called adjectives is because they are placed in the same position as adjectives and agree in case with the following noun. Otherwise, their endings are similar to those of nouns. They are only really used with nouns that describe animate objects and proper names. Never with nouns like <em>stůl, auto, mléko</em> (the Koho? Čeho? form is used instead).</p>
