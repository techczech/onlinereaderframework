<h1 id="reader-content-acknowledgements-and-licensing-details">Acknowledgements and licensing details</h1>
<h2 id="reader-content-origins-of-the-navigator">Origins of the Navigator</h2>
<p>The Czech Language Navigator as a whole is an original creation based on materials developed for the US Peace Corps language program in the mid 1990s (referred to in previous editions as the Yellow Pages of the Czech Language). The Navigator adds to the cumulative grammatical knowledge of many generations.</p>
<p>It is impossible to compile an entire grammar book completely from scratch. The information in this Navigator is based on the reframing of information from reference materials too numerous to list. Some portions of this text contain original analysis (most significantly aspect) and all explanations are original. But the main contribution is in the compilation. Many people contributed to the original drafts over a period of several years.</p>
<p>Some of the sections were composed as separate handouts over many years of teaching and therefore, there is a certain inconsistency of presentation and tone of language. It is possible that this also resulted in some small portions of the text being copied from elsewhere without attribution as is common in the practice of busy teachers putting together handouts. We tried to replace all of these with original work but some may have been overlooked. We will be happy to remove these when pointed out or provide attribution.</p>
