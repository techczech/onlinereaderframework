<h2 id="reader-content-space---kde-nebo-kam">Space - <em>Kde?</em> nebo <em>Kam?</em></h2>
<p>Czech distinguishes between locality and directionality rather religiously. All prepositions of space observe this distinction. It is important whether the object is standing still or moving. For instance: <em>auto jede na hory</em> (a car is going to the mountains) vs. <em>auto jede na horách</em> ( car is in the mountains). What tell these two sentences apart is the case ending.</p>
<p>There are two prepositions for each meaning ( e.g. <em>in</em> vs. <em>into</em>). One set uses the case to distinguish while the other set uses two different prepositions.</p>
