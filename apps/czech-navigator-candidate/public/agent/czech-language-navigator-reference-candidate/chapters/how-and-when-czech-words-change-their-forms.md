<p><strong>*</strong> Outside this system lie Conjunctions (and, if, or), Interjections (ouch!) and Particles (thus) which never change their form or influence other words to change form. <strong>Note:</strong> Some pronouns function as conjunctions (who, which, whose – so called relative pronouns) and of course change their form with all the categories relevant to pronouns.</p>
<p><strong>**</strong> Masculine nouns and adjectives can be either animate or inanimate thus in effect multiplying the number of genders to four.</p>
